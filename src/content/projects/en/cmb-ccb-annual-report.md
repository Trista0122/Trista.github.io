## Problem

China Merchants Bank is often called the "king of retail banking", while China Construction Bank is known for corporate and infrastructure lending. **How does this difference in positioning show up in the key numbers of their annual reports?** Which differences come from strategy, and which from scale and ownership?

## What I learned

- Reading the MD&A first tells you the story the bank wants to tell; the statements are where you check it.
- A single metric means little on its own — it needs peers and a time series.
- A bank's income statement is unlike a normal company's: interest income *is* revenue, and provisions are key to understanding swings in profit.
